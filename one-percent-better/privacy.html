Privacy Policy

1% Better collects no personal data. Your habits stay on your phone.

What the app stores

The app saves your habits, your wake-up and lights-out times, and which habits you ticked off each day. This is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. 1% Better has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests to us or to third parties.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app asks your phone's permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. No information leaves your phone to make them work.

Backups

When you tap "Save backup", the app creates a file with your habit data and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where that file goes, for example the Files app or a message to yourself. We never receive it.

Your phone's own backups

If you use iCloud Backup or Google backup, your phone may include the app's data in that backup, stored in your own account under Apple's or Google's privacy terms. We have no access to it.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Erase everything in the app, or delete the app. Either removes all of your data from the device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

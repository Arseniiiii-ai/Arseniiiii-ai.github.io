Support

Need help with 1% Better? Here are quick answers, and a way to reach us.

I'm not getting reminders

Open the app, go to Settings → Reminders and tap Turn on reminders. If notifications were turned off earlier, open your phone's Settings, find 1% Better and turn on Notifications. Reminders only go out for habits that have a time and Remind me turned on.

How do I move to a new phone?

On your old phone, go to Settings → Save backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install 1% Better and tap Settings → Restore backup.

Can I change my habits?

Yes. In Settings → Your habits, tap a habit to rename it, change its time or area, turn its reminder on or off, or remove it. Use Add a habit to pick from the list or create your own.

What counts as a good day?

Ticking off at least two-thirds of your habits. Good days build your streak and your compound score.
